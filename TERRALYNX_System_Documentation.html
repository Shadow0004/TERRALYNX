1. Executive Summary & Problem Space

Coastal and delta regions in eastern India—specifically the state of Odisha encompassing the Mahanadi River Basin, Cuttack Millennium City, and Bhubaneswar—are globally among the most vulnerable areas to hydrometeorological disasters. The convergence of Category 4 and 5 super cyclones, extreme cloudbursts (exceeding 250mm/24h), low-elevation coastal storm surges (1.5m to 4.5m), and high population densities in non-engineered (kutcha) housing frequently overwhelms civil infrastructure.

Historically, disaster response mechanisms have relied on reactive crisis mitigation—dispatching relief only after storm surge inundation occurs, relying on static manual census registers, and utilizing estimated aggregate district quotas that fail to pinpoint exact sub-ward sectors or dynamic road washouts.

The TERRALYNX Paradigm Shift: TERRALYNX transforms emergency response into a predictive, real-time, deterministic logistics command system. By fusing real-time Open-Meteo meteorological telemetry with dynamic OpenStreetMap (OSM) administrative sectors, hydraulic elevation models, and capacity-constrained linear optimization, TERRALYNX autonomously sizes evacuation fleets, allocates safe shelters, plans flood-free convoy corridors, and detects supply deficits hours before storm landfall.

3. Mathematical Modeling & Algorithmic Formulations

3.1 Multi-Factor Zone Vulnerability & Risk Index

Each administrative sector $z \in Z$ (e.g. CDA Sector 9, Bidanasi, Mahanadi Delta) is assigned a normalized risk score $R(z) \in [0, 100]$:

$$R(z) = 0.28 \cdot \min\left(100, rac{P_{24h}}{3.0} ight) + 0.22 \cdot \min\left(100, rac{W_{ ext{gusts}}}{2.0} ight) + 0.20 \cdot \min\left(100, S_{ ext{surge}} \cdot 25 ight) + 0.18 \cdot \max\left(0, 100 - E_{ ext{elev}} \cdot 10 ight) + 0.12 \cdot K_{ ext{kutcha}}$$

Where $P_{24h}$ is forecasted 24h rainfall in mm, $W_{ ext{gusts}}$ is wind gusts in km/h, $S_{ ext{surge}}$ is coastal surge height in meters, $E_{ ext{elev}}$ is mean sector elevation in meters, and $K_{ ext{kutcha}}$ is the percentage of kutcha/slum dwellings.

3.2 Evacuation Requirement Model

The mandatory evacuation demand $E(z)$ represents the exact count of citizens requiring assisted transfer to certified shelters:

$$E(z) = ext{Population}(z) imes ext{ExposureMultiplier}(R(z)) imes \left(0.40 \cdot rac{K_{ ext{kutcha}}}{100} + 0.35 \cdot ext{ElderlyRatio} + 0.25 \cdot ext{FloodThreatIndex} ight)$$

3.3 Capacity-Constrained Shelter Optimization

Evacuees from threatened sectors are allocated to safe shelters $S$ using a multi-factor linear distance-safety objective:

$$ ext{Cost}(z, s) = ext{HaversineDist}(z, s) + 0.10 \cdot (100 - ext{SafetyScore}(s)) - 0.20 \cdot \max(0, ext{Elevation}(s) - 5.0 ext{m})$$

The optimizer strictly enforces $\sum_{z} ext{Allocated}(z, s) \le ext{Capacity}(s)$. When utilization exceeds $80\%$, the system automatically queues Temporary Emergency Shelter Complexes (e.g. University Convention Centers, Indoor Stadiums).

3.4 Emergency Fleet Logistics Sizing

Logistical requirements and supply chain deficits are modeled as follows:

  • Evacuation Transit Buses: $ ext{Buses}_{ ext{req}} = \left\lceil rac{E_{ ext{total}} imes 0.80}{40 imes 1.5} ight ceil$ (40-pax capacity, turnaround factor 1.5).
  • Inflatable Rescue Boats & OBMs: $ ext{Boats}_{ ext{req}} = 4 imes N_{ ext{delta\_zones}} + 2 imes N_{ ext{critical\_zones}}$ (sectors with elevation $\le 3.5 ext{m}$).
  • Advance Life Support Ambulances: $ ext{Ambulances}_{ ext{req}} = \left\lceil rac{ ext{MedicalDependencies}}{18} ight ceil + N_{ ext{hospitals}}$.
  • NDRF / ODRAF Tactical Teams: $ ext{Teams}_{ ext{req}} = \left\lceil rac{E_{ ext{critical}}}{650} ight ceil + N_{ ext{critical\_zones}}$ (10-person units).
  • 72-Hour Sustenance Rations: $ ext{Rations}_{ ext{req}} = E_{ ext{total}} imes 3$ days packs.

4. Detailed Specification of the 7 Core Modules

1. 🚨 Incident Command Center
Real-time threat status banner, CAP emergency cell broadcast trigger, live telemetry metrics, and prioritized action directives with 1-click bulk authorization across multi-agency forces (ODRAF, NDRF, District Police).
2. 🗺️ Geospatial Risk & Weather Map
Sub-kilometer precision search (e.g. C. V. Raman University, CDA Sec 9), live Google Weather Card with rotating wind direction azimuths, 24h hourly forecast timelines, NullSchool wind stream particles, and official Census HUD.
3. 🏛️ Shelter & Camp Planner
Real-time tracking of capacity, current occupancy, incoming allocated evacuees, structural safety certifications, backup diesel power, and automatic activation of university stadiums upon overflow.
4. 🛣️ Dynamic Evacuation Routing
Evaluates road elevation profiles against hydraulic runoff and storm surge to calculate countdown timers before cutoff and dynamically routes convoys through elevated arterial bypasses.
5. 🚚 Resource Logistics Matrix
Supply chain health dashboard (Mobility, Water Rescue, Medical Triage, 72h Sustenance), shortfall tracking, and interactive mutual-aid requisition modal for instant asset dispatch.
6. 🧪 What-If Scenario Simulator
Interactive sliders (Rainfall $0.5 imes-2.2 imes$, Wind Cat 1-5, Surge $0.5-4.5$m), 1-click disaster presets (Cat-5, Cloudburst, Bridge Washout), and a ✨ Auto-Mitigate Engine with before/after differential analysis.
7. 🤖 Grounded AI Decision Assistant
Natural-language disaster intelligence interface powered by deterministic telemetry injection. Answers tactical questions regarding convoy dispatch windows, hospital evacuation readiness, and resource allocations with zero hallucinations.
